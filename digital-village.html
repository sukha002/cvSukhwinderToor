REMOTE SENSING

Sentinel-2
& NDVI

Sentinel-2 imagery was explored as a source of information about vegetation conditions across agricultural areas.

Vegetation indices were applied to imagery to examine vegetation patterns over time and across selected plots.

The documented analysis identified non-uniform NDVI patterns within selected plots that could indicate locations requiring additional field investigation.

NDVI

Vegetation Monitoring

Satellite-based vegetation analysis can help identify areas that may require closer examination using field measurements or additional remote-sensing methods.
