RESEARCH OVERVIEW

AI-Based
Food Quality
Detection

The research investigated machine learning and deep learning approaches for food-quality detection.

The documented work included fruit image collection and classification, together with an experiment involving classification of raw and adulterated milk samples.

EXPERIMENT 01

Fruit Image
Classification

Dataset Collection

Images of fruits such as apples and oranges were collected from different fruit vendors.

The images included different viewing angles and backgrounds.

CNN Model

A convolutional neural network was developed for fruit classification using the collected image dataset.

A graphical user interface was also documented for the fruit classifier.

PROPOSED RESEARCH DIRECTION

Visual +
Volatile
Features

The research material proposed combining visual characteristics with volatile compounds and other measurable characteristics.

Proposed inputs included visual features such as colour, shape, size and texture, together with compounds such as ethylene, carbon dioxide and oxygen.

Machine learning and deep learning models could then be trained to predict fruit ripeness and quality.
